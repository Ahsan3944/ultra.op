# Facial Photos

This is the dedicated creator-photo shelf for reusable facial and portrait photography.

Suggested subfolders:
- `portraits/` — clean headshots and profile photos.
- `closeups/` — close facial crops.
- `expressions/` — reactions and expressive photos for thumbnails.
- `press/` — polished photos intended for media or business use.

Preferred formats: JPG, JPEG, PNG or WEBP. Keep original high-resolution files when possible.
