Privacy
Policy.

Last updated: 11 September 2026

In short: the public UltraOP website is designed primarily as an informational creator site. It does not ask visitors to create an account or intentionally request sensitive personal information on the homepage.

1. Information you provide

If you contact UltraOP by email, the message may include your name, email address, company or organisation, and any other information you choose to provide. That information is used to understand and respond to the enquiry.

If you voluntarily send support through the displayed UPI details, payment processing is handled by the relevant payment provider or bank. UltraOP does not need your banking credentials to display the UPI ID or QR code.

2. Information handled by the website

The site uses normal browser functionality to display pages and remember temporary navigation state during a visit. For example, the website may use session storage for static-host routing recovery and browser clipboard access only when you actively choose to copy the displayed UPI ID.

As with most websites, the hosting infrastructure, CDN, browser and network providers may generate technical logs such as IP address, request time, user agent and requested resource. Those logs are generally controlled by the relevant infrastructure provider rather than by the website's creator interface.

3. YouTube and other external services

The website embeds or links to third-party services including YouTube, Instagram, Twitch, Kick, Rooter, Discord and WhatsApp. When you open or interact with those services, they may collect information according to their own privacy policies and settings.

The homepage also uses a public RSS-to-JSON service to retrieve the public YouTube feed for the Ultra OP Live card. This is used to select the latest non-Short video when a live stream is not available. Requests to that service may be logged by the service provider.

4. Cookies and tracking

The current public site does not require an account, login or advertising profile. It does not intentionally ask for sensitive personal information through the homepage. Third-party embeds, hosting infrastructure or linked platforms may use their own cookies or similar technologies when their content is loaded or visited.

5. How information is used

Information supplied directly to UltraOP is used for legitimate creator, business, collaboration, support or website-related communication. It is not intentionally collected through the public site for unrelated marketing purposes.

6. Sharing

UltraOP does not intentionally sell personal information submitted through the website. Information may be handled by service providers where necessary to host the website, deliver external content, process a communication or provide a third-party platform feature.

7. External links

Links from this website can take you to third-party websites and social platforms. Once you leave ultraop.in, the destination's own privacy policy and terms apply. Review those policies before providing personal information.

8. Data requests and questions

If you have a privacy question or believe you have provided personal information that needs attention, contact ultraopbiz@gmail.com with enough detail for the request to be understood. Where applicable, requests are handled subject to legal, security and operational requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, integrations or creator services change. The latest version published here explains the practices applicable to the current public site.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: ultraopbiz@gmail.com.
